Features you already have

You can already do more than you think

01

Own your data

Move it, host it, or keep it entirely on your device. You decide where every reading lives.

Export analysis-ready data CSV, delivered as a ZIP by email or a session link.

Open a completed session in the AirCasting app or on aircasting.org and choose export. Your data arrives as a ZIP of CSV files, by email or as a shareable session link.

Keep your data in-house The backend is open source, and the app can point at your own server.

Deploy the open-source AirCasting backend on your own server and point the app at it, so sessions stay in-house. For a single session you can also record fully on the device and share it as a file, only with the people you choose.

02

Shape the session

Tune how AirBeam records and let it tell you when conditions change.

Control your sampling rate Match co-deployed instruments and manage data volume precisely.

Set the recording interval in the AirCasting app before you start a session, so the Mini matches your other instruments and keeps data volume manageable. Available on the AirBeam Mini with current firmware. Earlier AirBeam models don't support it.

Set alerts, get emailed Choose a threshold in the app and get notified when it is crossed.

In the app, set a threshold on the measurement you care about. When a reading crosses it, you get an email — no need to watch the screen.

03

See the bigger picture

Put your readings in context with nearby monitors and the public map.

Follow other monitors Track nearby fixed and government sessions alongside your own.

Search the map for a nearby fixed or government session and follow it, so it appears alongside your own readings for comparison.

See it all on the map Explore the full public air-quality map on aircasting.org.

Open aircasting.org for every public session worldwide. In the app you get your own sessions plus nearby outdoor fixed and government monitors, so you can compare on the go.
