Features you already have

You can already do more than you think.

Here is what ships today.

Export analysis-ready data

CSV and Excel, delivered as a ZIP by email or a session link.

How to export a session

Open a completed session in the AirCasting app or on aircasting.org and choose export. You receive a ZIP with CSV and Excel files by email or as a shareable session link.

Keep your data in-house

The backend is open source, and the app can point at your own server, so sessions stay off AirCasting. Plan for one IT step, or follow our setup guide.

How self-hosting works

Deploy the open-source AirCasting backend on your own server and point the app at it. It takes one IT step; our setup guide walks through it. Turning off crowd-map contribution keeps a session unlisted, not private — for true privacy, self-host.

Control your sampling rate

Set how often the Mini records, to match co-deployed instruments and manage data volume.

Where to set the sampling rate

Set the recording interval in the AirCasting app before you start a session, so the Mini matches your other instruments and keeps data volume manageable.

Set alerts, get emailed

Set a threshold in the app and get notified by email.

How to set an alert

In the app, set a threshold on the measurement you care about. When a reading crosses it, you get an email — no need to watch the screen.

Follow other monitors

Track nearby fixed and government sessions alongside your own.

How to follow a monitor

Search the map for a nearby fixed or government session and follow it, so it appears alongside your own readings for comparison.

See it all on the map

aircasting.org shows the full public map. The app shows your sessions plus nearby outdoor fixed and government sessions.

Open the map

Open aircasting.org for every public session worldwide. In the app you get your own sessions plus nearby outdoor fixed and government monitors, so you can compare on the go.
